Ban List

Banned

A card is banned when it is one of the 50 most played cards of a competitive format, when it is banned in one of those formats anyway, or when it is restricted in Vintage.

Cards considered Extended

These show up in the top 150 of a competitive format. They are fine in a normal game, but they are out as well if you play with the extended ban list.

Every season is calculated from scratch, so the lists look different next time. Back to the API landing page, or over to the format homepage.
